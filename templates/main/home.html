What We Detect

Attentive

Students actively participating, making eye contact, and showing interest.

Sleepy

Signs of fatigue: drooping eyelids, yawning, or resting heads.

Distracted

Looking away, using phones, or not following the lesson.

Neutral

Standard resting state – neither highly engaged nor clearly disengaged.
