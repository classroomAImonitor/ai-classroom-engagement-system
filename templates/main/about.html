📖 Our Project Vision

Harnessing the power of Computer Vision to bridge the gap between digital analytics and physical classroom engagement.

🎯 Our Mission

The AI-Driven Classroom Engagement Monitoring System was conceptualized to address a core challenge in modern education: the difficulty of tracking real-time attention levels in large classroom settings.

Our mission is to provide educators with a non-intrusive, privacy-first tool that translates student micro-behaviors into actionable data, allowing for early intervention and curriculum optimization.

🧠

The Neural Core

Powered by OpenCV and MediaPipe, our system analyzes 468 facial landmarks to determine engagement states with high precision.

🌟 Core Principles

Privacy First

On-premise processing ensures that student video data never leaves the institution's local network.

Data-Driven

Moving from intuition-based teaching to evidence-based pedagogical adjustments.

Student-Centric

Designed to help identify students who may be struggling with fatigue or lack of interest early on.
